Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At OREXON LUXE, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our clients, partners, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or engage with our B2B services.

01 Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out forms, request quotes, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact our B2B team. This may include:

  • Contact details such as your name, business email address, phone number, and company name.
  • Property information including hotel name, location, number of rooms, and procurement requirements.
  • Communication records such as inquiries, feedback, and correspondence with our team.
  • Technical data including IP address, browser type, device information, and browsing behaviour on our site.

02 How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to your inquiries and providing tailored product recommendations.
  • Processing orders, managing accounts, and coordinating logistics and delivery.
  • Sending important updates, invoices, and after-sales communications.
  • Improving our website, product offerings, and customer experience.
  • Complying with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is shared only with trusted partners who assist us in delivering our services, and only under strict confidentiality agreements.

03 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including SSL encryption, secure servers, and restricted access controls, to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

04 Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and understand user behaviour. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of our website.

05 Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

06 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

Shipping & Returns

Last Updated: September 2026

We are committed to delivering your products safely, efficiently, and on time. This policy outlines our shipping methods, delivery timelines, and return procedures for B2B clients across India.

01 Shipping Methods & Timelines

We ship across India using trusted freight and courier partners. Delivery timelines vary by destination and order volume:

Destination Standard Delivery Express Delivery
Within Uttar Pradesh 2–4 business days 1–2 business days
North India 3–6 business days 2–3 business days
West & Central India 5–8 business days 3–4 business days
South India 6–10 business days 3–5 business days
North East & Remote Areas 8–14 business days 5–7 business days

02 Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated based on order volume, weight, destination, and chosen shipping method. A detailed shipping quote will be provided with your formal quotation. For bulk orders over a certain threshold, we may offer complimentary shipping to select destinations within India.

03 Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive a tracking number and a link to monitor your shipment in real time. Our logistics team is available to assist with any tracking or customs inquiries throughout the delivery process.

04 Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be fully satisfied with your purchase. If you receive a product that is damaged, defective, or incorrect, please contact us within 5 business days of delivery. Our return policy includes:

  • Damaged on arrival: Report within 5 days with photographic evidence for a free replacement or full refund.
  • Defective products: Covered under our warranty for repair or replacement.
  • Incorrect items shipped: We will arrange free return shipping and send the correct items immediately.
  • Change of mind: Returns accepted within 14 days for unopened, unused items in original packaging, subject to a 15% restocking fee.

Custom-manufactured and personalized products are non-returnable unless they arrive damaged or defective. Please review all custom specifications carefully before order confirmation.

05 Return Process

To initiate a return, please contact our support team at orexonluxe@gmail.com with your order number and reason for return. Our team will provide a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and detailed instructions. Returns sent without an RMA number may not be accepted.

Warranty

Last Updated: September 2026

Every OREXON LUXE product is backed by our commitment to quality and craftsmanship. We offer a comprehensive warranty program designed to give our B2B clients complete peace of mind.

01 Standard Warranty Coverage

All OREXON LUXE products come with a 6-month comprehensive warranty from the date of delivery, covering manufacturing defects, material faults, and functional failures under normal use. Mini Fridges come with a 1-year warranty on the cooling system and compressor.

Product Category Warranty Period Coverage
Smart Toilets 6 months Full parts & labour
Hand Dryers 6 months Motor & electronics
Hair Dryers 6 months Motor & heating element
Soap Dispensers 6 months Sensor & pump mechanism
Sensor Taps 6 months Valve & sensor assembly
Mini Fridges 1 year Cooling system & compressor
Landline Phones 6 months Electronics & components

02 What's Covered

  • Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.
  • Electrical or mechanical failures under normal operating conditions.
  • Component malfunctions that affect product functionality.
  • Free repair or replacement at our discretion, including parts and labour.

03 What's Not Covered

  • Damage caused by improper installation, misuse, or negligence.
  • Normal wear and tear, cosmetic scratches, or minor discolouration.
  • Damage from power surges, water damage, or unauthorized repairs.
  • Products modified or altered without our written approval.
  • Consumable parts such as filters, batteries, or seals.

04 Extended Warranty Plans

For high-traffic commercial properties and long-term installations, we offer extended warranty plans that provide up to 3 years of coverage, including priority support, on-site service visits, and guaranteed replacement parts availability.

05 How to Claim Warranty

To make a warranty claim, please follow these steps:

  • Contact our support team at orexonluxe@gmail.com with your order number and product details.
  • Provide a description of the issue along with photographs or video evidence.
  • Our technical team will review your claim within 2 business days.
  • If approved, we will arrange repair, replacement, or refund at no cost to you.

Our promise: We stand behind every product we sell. If something goes wrong within the warranty period, we'll make it right — quickly, professionally, and without hassle.
