Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Inquiries

What products does Orexon Luxe offer?

Orexon Luxe offers premium hotel supplies including mini bar fridges, automatic hand dryers, sensor faucets, automatic soap dispensers, smart toilets, hair dryers, electric kettles, landline telephones and other luxury hospitality essentials.

Do you supply products for hotels and large projects?

Yes. We cater to hotels, resorts, commercial spaces, luxury residences and hospitality projects, with solutions suitable for both individual requirements and bulk orders.

Can I request a customized quotation for my project?

Absolutely. Share your product requirements, quantity and project details with us, and our team will provide a tailored quotation based on your specific needs.

Do you provide product support after purchase?

Yes. We believe our relationship with clients goes beyond the sale. Our team assists with product guidance, installation-related coordination and after-sales support as applicable.

Why choose Orexon Luxe for luxury hotel supplies?

Because luxury is not just about appearance — it is about quality, functionality and consistency. Orexon Luxe brings carefully selected hospitality products together with professional service and project-focused solutions.
